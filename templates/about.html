Our Story

Meet Chinni

Chinni started as a mason over 15 years ago, learning the craft from the ground up. With dedication, hard work, and an eye for quality, he grew from a single worker into a trusted contractor leading a skilled team.

Today, Chinni's Construction handles everything from full home construction (1, 2, and 3 BHK) to compound walls, round tanks, Elevations and all types of mason work. We also rent construction materials like centering sticks, iron sheets, boxes, and more.

Our mission is simple: deliver honest, high-quality construction work that families and businesses can rely on for generations.
